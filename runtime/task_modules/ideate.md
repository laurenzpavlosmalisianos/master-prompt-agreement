# Runtime Task: Ideate

Use for open-ended design or solution exploration.

Consult `STATEMENT_OF_WORK.md`, `TODO.md`, `DECISIONS.md`, or `PRECEDENTS.md` when authority, approval, current state, durable decisions, or recorded triggers can affect the task.

Switch to the full Task Order when external sources, living standards, or unresolved evidence tradeoffs matter.

1. State the problem, constraints, and evaluation criteria.
2. Generate multiple competing approaches before ranking them.
3. Record supporting evidence, contradicting evidence, and unknowns for each.
4. Check for anchoring, confirmation bias, availability bias, and sunk cost.
5. Recommend one option or a concrete hybrid with stated confidence.
6. Classify the chosen direction by ownership. Route separately authorized
   downstream or project-local planning to `plan`; route a shared or reusable
   framework-product candidate to `framework_semantic_audit`.

Checks:

- two or three materially distinct viable approaches were considered by default, or the single viable approach is justified
- evidence for and against each approach is explicit
- confidence is justified
- the recommendation follows from the criteria
- any chosen direction follows its ownership-specific next route
